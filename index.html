// questions

Before you ask.

Do you work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. If it has an API, a webhook, a spreadsheet export or even just a web page, I can connect it. We confirm this on the audit call before you pay anything.

Who owns the accounts, ads and code?

You do. Your Google profile, ad accounts, servers and code stay in your name. I work with manager access and hand over the full source plus documentation.

Is ad spend included in the ads price?

No. You pay Google and Meta directly from your own ad account, so every dollar of spend is visible to you. My monthly fee covers setup, tracking and management.

How do payments work?

Fixed-price builds are paid half upfront and half on delivery; monthly services are billed at the start of each month. You can pay in USDT, USDC, SOL, BTC, ETH, through Binance Pay, or with Whish.

What if something breaks after launch?

Bugs in what I built are fixed free for 30 days after launch. After that, the care plan covers monitoring and fixes, or I quote one-off changes.

Can you guarantee trading profits?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. I build reliable execution and risk tooling around your strategy; the strategy and the risk stay yours.
